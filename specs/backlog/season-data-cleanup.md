# Season data cleanup

Prompt for `/speckit.specify`:

"Specify what happens to the past season's data at the season rollover. After the
maintainer has made the season albums (feature 021) and the organisers have set
the new season start (1 September), decide which stored data from before the new
season start is kept and which is deleted: ride results, runs, swims, team events
and attendances, participation reports (feature 019), corrections, earned badges
and the balances. Weigh data minimisation (constitution Principle I) against
anything a later feature needs (e.g. comparing seasons, carry-over badges). Say
who triggers the cleanup, whether it can be undone, and what riders and organisers
see after it."

Depends on feature 021 (season albums are made before any cleanup).
