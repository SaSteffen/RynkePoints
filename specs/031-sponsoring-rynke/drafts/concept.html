Sponsoring-Rynke

A third Rynke category next to Training and Team. It rewards asking and closing, shows the team's money as its own figure, and gets people started. It never counts toward qualifying for the tour.

Concept

Decisions

  • Everyone is in. No opt-in, since this is core team business and riders already see these figures.
  • Rynke are the main number, because they also cover actions that bring in no money. Euros appear beside them as their own metric.
  • Closing a company counts more than its amount. €1,000 is about as hard to win as €250.
  • Team sponsorship money is its own figure. It never counts toward the charity collection. A rider who wins one earns dampened Rynke, and one with no rider behind it only adds to the team's figure.
  • Material sponsorship is rated by T-shirt size, and each size earns a set amount of Rynke.
  • Package and money are separate. The package chosen sets the Rynke, and the money earned only feeds the collection graph.
  • A sponsorship belongs to one team at full price, including Platin and Platin Plus, whose perks are shared across teams.
  • No percentages. Last season's money is a comparison line, and a setting decides whether riders see it.
  • Only finalised money counts. There are no refunds.
  • The season runs 1 September to 31 August, whenever the riding evaluation starts.
  • The organiser page shows a count of riders who have started, with no "needs a push" list.
  • Sponsors are the six packages, Bronze (€500) to Platin Plus (€25,000). The Rynke per package stay configurable.
  • A contact is yes or no. The sheet lists only real touchpoints, such as mail, social media or a call.
  • The ranking stays nameless, like the other two views.

What follows

  • "Started" means at least one contact, or any money or sponsorship credited, this season.
  • A mixed team means a mixed page. Newcomers see a first step, veterans see their figures and how to bring others along. The app picks the view from the rider's figures, so nobody has to choose a type.
  • Quiet people get a route too. Some tips work without cold calling: the employer's donation process, a short email, the rider's own network.
  • No new bottom-nav tab. It lives in the Team tab, on Du, on Abzeichen and in Orga.
  • The word: "Sponsoringrynke" in the app, matching Trainingsrynke and Teamrynke.

Scoring

The Rynke come from a plain table with one value per package chosen. What the sponsor actually paid doesn't affect them and only feeds the collection graph. The square root of the price gave the starting values, but the app needs no formula for them: Platin costs 25 times as much as Bronze and earns 5 times the Rynke.

Architecture

The app only ever sees a small, stable snapshot. Each season's messy sources stay in the job, so a new sheet layout changes one adapter and nothing in the app.

  • Team sheet of contacts
  • Campaign pages, if separate

  • One adapter per source
  • Fetches the rider list
  • Matches names locally
  • Checks, then pushes once a day

Snapshot endpoint

  • Token protected
  • Replaces the season's figures
  • Rejects unknown riders
  • Holds suspicious updates

The boundary: sponsor names and contact details stay on the job's side, and Cloudflare gets only counts and sums. Everyone being in is about riders' figures. Sponsors and their contact people are outsiders who never agreed to anything here, and the app doesn't need their names.

The snapshot

Scoring per sponsor needs each sponsor's package and amount, so rider totals aren't enough. ref is a keyed hash of the sponsor's name, made by the job. It stays the same from day to day and can't be read back.

Material ratings are stored separately, so a new snapshot never overwrites an organiser's decision. They have no free-text note, so sponsor names can't slip in.

Rules that keep it dependable

  • Re-running is harmless. The same snapshot gives the same result, and the job can retry freely.
  • A drop means a mistake. With no refunds, totals only rise, so any fall holds the update for a person to check.
  • Unmatched names stay local. The job reports them to whoever runs it and sends only a count, which never holds an update by itself. Their money arrives as one anonymous sum, so the team total stays right. People in the intranet who aren't in the app never reach Cloudflare.
  • Leaving still deletes. A rider's sponsoring data goes with everything else, and a later push for that rider is refused.
  • Stale data is visible. Pages show "Stand", and admins get an alert after a few days without a push.

Changes to existing rules

  • Constitution: allow one outside job, and let points come from stored sponsoring figures as well as activities.
  • Consent text: the constitution asks riders again when the app newly shows something to others. With no opt-in, this becomes a one-time notice plus updated connect and privacy text, and the constitution needs a line that allows it.
  • Quotes: the rule "no quote is about fundraising" stays for today's lists. Sponsoring lines get their own lists.
  • Second team: sponsoring becomes a per-team setting with its own job.

Build order

  • First: endpoint, scoring, all screens, and a fake snapshot in pnpm dev.
  • Then: the job with the sheet adapter, run by hand.
  • Later: the intranet adapter and the daily schedule.
