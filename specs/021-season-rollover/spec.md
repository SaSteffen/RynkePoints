# Feature Specification: Season Rollover – Tour de Paris Badges and the Season Album

**Feature Branch**: `021-season-rollover`

**Created**: 2026-10-20

**Status**: Draft

**Mock-up**: Claude Design "RynkePoints Saisonalbum",
https://claude.ai/artifact/Cre5jJh2RSpA3RJJe4PuNn

**Input**: User description: "Let's plan the season rollover. At the end of the
season I just want people to have fun with the app: we need a team badge for every
day of the Tour de Paris (a separate delivery phase: once the route is known, I want
to create a special badge for each day of the tour that relates to the place; for
now we can have stand-in descriptions). The badges are awarded if people take part
in a new team event type 'Tour de Paris'. I think we only need one event type. If
someone has taken part twice at the event, the badge for day 2 is awarded (the
badge for day 1 is awarded the day before). Again it is a team badge, even though
the criterion is an individual's participation. Back to season rollover: at the
end of August of each year I want to create 'reports': a kind of sticker book that
shows the Rynke earned until then and summarises team events on the first two pages
or so. On the following pages it works like a Panini album: there is an empty
placeholder (we have those in the app) for every badge to be awarded, and the
earned badges are 'stickered' in, so their placement must not be perfect and it
looks a little more like a sticker album. The report generation is triggered by an
admin. We may make it downloadable through the app if that is possible within the
free tier limits of Cloudflare (the PDF might become large); check whether we can
generate it in a Cloudflare environment. I think that is possibly too much of a
burden, so generating it on a local machine by the app's admin is fine and within
the confines of GDPR: the files are placed in a temporary folder inside the repo
and then deleted by the admin manually. Self-service delivery via the app is a
concern for a future delivery phase, but DO research its feasibility. The format of
the Panini album is A4."

The album's look is agreed in the Claude Design mock-up "RynkePoints Saisonalbum"
(https://claude.ai/artifact/Cre5jJh2RSpA3RJJe4PuNn): four A4 pages of a synthetic rider "Anna" for the sample season 2026/27
with cut-off 31.08.2027 – page 1 (cover and her season, a right-hand page with
the gutter on the left), page 2 (the team's season and its event table, a
left-hand page with the gutter on the right), page 3 (her sticker page with
Distanz and Höhenmeter, earned and empty slots) and page 12 (team stickers:
all eight stand-in tour days and the first row of Team-Überraschungen with "?"
slots), plus the slot component in its three states. A "showSafe" switch on each
page shows the safe area and the binding gutter; they are not printed.

The feasibility research for self-service delivery is in
[research.md](research.md) (R1–R6).

## Delivery phases

- **Phase 1 (this spec)**: the "Tour de Paris" team-event kind, the team badge
  category "Tour de Paris" with stand-in names, descriptions and the existing team
  placeholder picture, and the season album generated by the maintainer on their
  own computer.
- **Phase 2 (later, once the tour route is known)**: for each of the 8 tour days
  a name, a description and a picture that relate to that day's place, drawn like
  feature 013's badges. It only swaps texts and pictures; it does not block Phase 1
  or Phase 3, and nothing else in this spec changes.
- **Never**: the albums are never generated in Cloudflare (owner, 2026-10-20).
- **Phase 3 (later)**: the albums stay built on the maintainer's computer, and
  the command uploads them to Cloudflare R2, from where each rider downloads
  their own album in the app. Not part of this spec; [research.md](research.md)
  R4 and R7 cover its feasibility. Phase 1 already keeps the albums ready for it
  (one file per rider, built the same way).
- **Out of scope**: what happens to the past season's data after the rollover
  (a later spec, see [backlog](../backlog/season-data-cleanup.md)).

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-10-20

- Q: How many event kinds does the tour need? → A: One, "Tour de Paris". Each tour
  day is one team event of that kind, as a training weekend is one event per day
  (feature 003 FR-006).
- Q: When is the badge for tour day n earned? → A: When one listed rider has
  attended n tour events of the season. A rider's first tour day earns "Tour-Tag 1",
  their second, the next day, "Tour-Tag 2", and so on. It is a team badge (feature 013
  FR-014): it is earned by the team and never names or credits the rider whose
  attendance earned it. (Recorded as Claude read the request; to be confirmed.)
- Q: Does this bring back the "Tour" category feature 013 dropped? → A: No. That
  category would have rewarded qualifying, a decision people make. This one rewards
  the team riding the tour, day by day.
- Q: Who gets an album? → A: Every listed rider (feature 014) at the time the
  album is made, organisers included; each album holds only that rider's own data
  and figures of the whole team.
- Q: In the album, do empty slots keep badges secret, as in the app? → A: No
  for ladder badges: an empty slot shows the badge's name, as a real sticker
  album prints it, now that the season is over. Yes for surprise badges
  ("Überraschungen", "Team-Überraschungen"): their empty slots show "?" only, as
  in the app (feature 013 FR-015b).
- Q: Do tour days count for other badge categories? → A: Yes, as every team event
  does: Team events attended, Big training and Attendances (feature 013 FR-010,
  FR-011), even though the tour lies after the qualification deadline. A badge not
  earned during the season can still be earned on the tour ("more fun that way").
  They still earn no Rynke. (Recorded from the owner's remark; the answer named
  option A, whose wording said the opposite.)
- Q: What happens to the past season's data after the albums are made? → A: Not
  part of this spec; it gets a spec of its own (backlog prompt
  `specs/backlog/season-data-cleanup.md`).
- Q: How many tour days are there, and does Phase 2 block anything? → A: The
  tour always has 8 days. The badges get stand-in names and texts now; Phase 2
  only swaps in place-related names, texts and pictures and blocks nothing.
- Q: How is the album made, and where? → A: As an HTML page printed to PDF by a
  headless browser on the maintainer's computer (research.md R1, R8). Generation
  never moves to Cloudflare.
- Q: Must the PDF be print-ready? → A: Yes: A4, margins with a binding gutter,
  vector wherever possible, at least 300 dpi otherwise (FR-050 to FR-057).
- Q: Phase 3: how do riders get their album from the app? → A: The albums are
  still built on the maintainer's computer; the command uploads them to
  Cloudflare R2, and riders download their own from the app. Phase 1 builds
  locally only, without upload (research.md R7).
- Q (raised by the project owner, on the mock-up): How should the stickers look?
  → A: Realistic, with a realistic shadow, and stuck onto the printed
  placeholders imperfectly, so a sticker sometimes leaves the placeholder's edge
  showing on one side, at random (deterministic per rider and badge, FR-026).
  No glitter (tried in the mock-up, dropped by the owner).

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

Terms as in features 003, 013, 014 and 019: **team event**, **attendance**,
**listed rider**, **organiser**, **badge**, **team badge**, **placeholder**. New
terms:

- A **tour day** is a team event of the kind "Tour de Paris".
- The **maintainer** is the person who runs the app (feature 014): the only one
  with access to the production data from their own computer. The request calls
  them the app's admin.
- A **season album** is one rider's A4 PDF made at the end of a season: two pages
  of summary, then **sticker pages** with one **slot** per badge of the catalog.
- A badge is **stuck in** when its picture fills its slot in the album, a little
  rotated and off-centre, as a sticker stuck in by hand.
- The **cut-off date** is the date the album shows the season up to, by default
  the day it is made, normally 31 August.

### User Story 1 - Organisers record the tour days (Priority: P1)

Before the tour an organiser creates one team event of the kind "Tour de Paris"
per tour day, with its date and, if they like, the day's stage as name ("Hamburg –
Bremen"). During or after each day they record who rode, as for any team event, and
riders can report it themselves on the Termine tab (feature 019).

**Why this priority**: Without recorded tour days there are no tour badges and
the album has no tour to show.

**Independent Test**: With `pnpm dev`, create tour days after the qualification
deadline as an organiser, record attendance, and check that the events are listed,
take attendance and change no rider's Rynke.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an organiser creating a team event, **When** they pick the kind,
   **Then** "Tour de Paris" is offered next to the three existing kinds.
2. **Given** a tour day dated after the qualification deadline, **When** it is
   saved, **Then** it is listed on the organiser's Termine page and on the riders'
   Termine tab and takes attendance from its date on (feature 014 FR-022).
3. **Given** a rider's attendance at a tour day, **When** balances are worked out,
   **Then** the rider's Training and Team Rynke and qualification do not change.
4. **Given** a tour day on the Termine tab, **When** a rider looks at it, **Then**
   it shows its date, kind and name and no Rynke amount, and offers "Bin dabei" or
   "Ich war dabei" as any other event (feature 019).

### User Story 2 - The team earns a badge for every tour day (Priority: P1)

The team badges get a new category, "Tour de Paris", with one badge per tour day:
"Tour-Tag 1", "Tour-Tag 2" and so on. When the first rider has their first tour day recorded,
the team earns "Tour-Tag 1"; when someone has their second recorded, "Tour-Tag 2". Like every
team badge it shows under "Team-Abzeichen" for every rider, with the date of the
tour day that earned it, and the progress line says how many tour days are still
to go ("Noch 3 Tage").

**Why this priority**: The tour is the high point of the season; a badge a day
makes it visible in the app for everyone, also for riders at home.

**Independent Test**: With synthetic tour days, record one rider on two of them
and another rider on one; check that "Tour-Tag 1" and "Tour-Tag 2" are earned with the right
dates, that no badge names a rider, and that the progress shows the days to go.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** no tour attendance, **When** a rider opens their badges, **Then**
   "Tour de Paris" shows as a team category with an empty outline and "Noch 8
   Tage" (the tour has 8 days).
2. **Given** Anna attended the tour days of 20 and 21 June, **When** badges are
   worked out, **Then** the team has "Tour-Tag 1" dated 20 June and "Tour-Tag 2" dated 21
   June.
3. **Given** Anna attended one tour day and Ben another one, **When** badges are
   worked out, **Then** the team has "Tour-Tag 1" only, dated with the earlier day.
4. **Given** an attendance at a tour day is removed, **When** badges are worked
   out again, **Then** a day badge no rider reaches any more is taken back
   silently (feature 013 FR-031).
5. **Given** a rider attended more tour days than the catalog has, **When**
   badges are worked out, **Then** the last day badge is earned and nothing more.
6. **Given** a new tour badge, **When** it is earned, **Then** each listed rider
   is told once, like any new team badge (feature 013 FR-032), without the name of
   the rider who earned it.

### User Story 3 - The maintainer makes the season albums (Priority: P1)

At the end of August the maintainer runs one command on their own computer. It
reads the season from the production data, read-only, and writes one A4 PDF per
listed rider into a folder inside the repository that git ignores. The maintainer
hands each rider their own album, then deletes the folder. The command says how
many albums it made, where they are, and that the folder must be deleted once they
are handed out.

**Why this priority**: The album is the season's keepsake; making it locally keeps
it within the free tier and needs nothing new in production.

**Independent Test**: With synthetic data (`pnpm dev`'s sample season), run the
command against the local database; check one PDF per listed rider, the folder's
place, that git ignores it, that nothing in the database changed and that no Strava
request was made.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** 14 listed riders and 2 riders who are not listed, **When** the
   maintainer runs the command, **Then** 14 albums are written, one per listed
   rider, and none for the others.
2. **Given** the albums were written, **When** `git status` runs, **Then** the
   folder and its files do not show.
3. **Given** the command ran, **When** the production data is compared with
   before, **Then** nothing changed and no Strava request was made.
4. **Given** the same data and the same cut-off date, **When** the command runs
   twice, **Then** the albums look the same (the stickers sit in the same places).
5. **Given** a cut-off date before the season's end, **When** the command runs
   with it, **Then** every figure and badge in the album is as of that date.
6. **Given** the organisers already moved the season start to the new season,
   **When** the maintainer runs the command, **Then** it stops and says the
   albums must be made before the season start moves.

### User Story 4 - The album's first pages sum up the season (Priority: P1)

The album opens with the rider's season: the cover with "Rynke-Album" (stand-in
title), the season ("Saison 2026/27") and the rider's first name, the rider's
Training and Team Rynke at the cut-off date with the coin, and how many team events
they attended. The next page sums up the team's season: the team's Training Rynke
together, every team event of the season by date with its kind, name and how many
riders attended, the rider's own attendances ticked, and the tour days.

**Why this priority**: The summary turns the badge pages into the story of the
season, for the rider and for the team.

**Independent Test**: Make an album for a synthetic rider and compare every figure
on the first two pages with the rider's page and the Team page of the app at the
cut-off date.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** Anna with 312 Training and 31 Team Rynke at the cut-off date,
   **When** her album is made, **Then** page 1 shows both, her first name and the
   season.
2. **Given** 24 team events in the season, **When** page 2 is made, **Then** it
   lists all 24 with date, kind, name and number of riders, Anna's attendances
   ticked.
3. **Given** more team events than fit one page, **When** the album is made,
   **Then** the list goes on to the next page, before the sticker pages.
4. **Given** any album, **When** its summary pages are read, **Then** they name
   no other rider and show no other rider's figures.

---

### User Story 5 - Sticker pages like a Panini album (Priority: P1)

After the summary, the sticker pages hold one slot for every badge of the catalog:
first the rider's own categories ("Meine Abzeichen"), then the team's
("Team-Abzeichen"), each category under its name, its badges in catalog order. An
slot has its placeholder printed on the page. An earned badge is stuck onto it
as a sticker: turned a little and off-centre, sometimes far enough that
the placeholder's edge shows on one side, with a realistic shadow and, now and
then, an edge that lifts a little; its name and date earned are printed below. A
badge not earned leaves the placeholder empty. Each
group says how many of its badges are stuck in ("42 von 87").

**Why this priority**: The empty slots between the stickers are what make it an
album, and a reason to collect more next season.

**Independent Test**: Make albums for synthetic riders with no, some and all
badges; check that every catalog badge has exactly one slot, earned ones stuck in,
the others empty, and that no sticker leaves its slot or the page.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the catalog's badges, **When** an album is made, **Then** each badge
   has exactly one slot, in the order of the app's gallery (feature 013 FR-002).
2. **Given** Anna earned "Distanz 1000", **When** her album is made, **Then** its
   slot shows the badge rotated by a few degrees and moved by a few millimetres,
   with its name and the date earned.
3. **Given** two earned badges side by side, **When** they are stuck in, **Then**
   they are rotated and moved differently from each other, and neither overlaps
   the other or leaves its slot.
4. **Given** a badge Anna did not earn, **When** her album is made, **Then** its
   slot shows the app's empty dotted outline, not a picture.
5. **Given** a ladder badge Anna did not earn, **When** its slot is shown,
   **Then** it shows the placeholder with the badge's name below it.
6. **Given** a surprise badge Anna did not earn, **When** its slot is shown,
   **Then** it shows the placeholder with "?" only.
7. **Given** the album, **When** it is printed on A4, **Then** nothing is cut off
   at the page edge.

### Edge Cases

- **Tour day before the qualification deadline** (e.g. a test ride named "Tour de
  Paris"): it still earns no Rynke and counts for badges like any tour day.
- **"Big training" on the tour**: a tour day with 20 riders earns the Big training
  badges the team has not reached yet, dated with that tour day.
- **Tour days in two seasons**: each season counts its own; the album shows the
  tour days of its season.
- **Tour day deleted**: its attendances go (feature 003 FR-006a); tour badges are
  worked out again.
- **Rider who joined during the season**: their album shows what they earned; team
  badges earned before they joined are in their album, as in the app (feature 013
  US4).
- **Rider who left before the albums are made**: their data is gone (feature 001
  FR-022); there is no album for them, and they count only where team figures
  already counted them before (feature 013 FR-032).
- **Rider who must reconnect or has no current consent**: not listed (feature 004
  FR-021), no album.
- **Rider who is an organiser**: gets an album like everyone else.
- **Rider with no badge**: the sticker pages are all empty slots; the album is
  still made.
- **Season without tour days**: the tour category's slots are all empty; page 2
  says there were no tour days yet.
- **Badge earned after the cut-off date**: not in the album.
- **Catalog changed during the season** (feature 013 FR-012): the album uses the
  catalog of the release it is made with.
- **Phase 2 replaces names and pictures after badges were earned**: earned tour
  badges stay earned with their dates; they only show the new name, description
  and picture.
- **Riders with the same first name**: each album carries only its own rider's
  name; file names keep them apart without anything beyond the first name and a
  number.
- **The command is interrupted**: albums already written stay; running it again
  writes all of them anew.
- **Maintainer forgets to delete the folder**: the command, when run, says how old
  the albums in the folder are and that they must be deleted (FR-034).

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

**Tour de Paris event kind (amends features 003, 014, 019)**

- **FR-001**: Team events MUST get a fourth kind, "Tour de Paris" (feature 003
  FR-006). One tour day is one event of that kind.
- **FR-002**: Attendance at a tour day MUST earn no Training and no Team Rynke and
  MUST NOT count towards qualification (feature 003 FR-007, FR-013). The fixed
  amounts per kind (feature 003 FR-012) MUST NOT offer amounts for this kind.
- **FR-003**: Tour days MUST be limited by the season start only, not by the
  qualification deadline (an exception to feature 003 FR-011): from the season
  start on they are listed, take attendance (feature 014 FR-022) and take reports
  (feature 019).
- **FR-004**: Wherever events show what attendance earns (feature 019 FR-002), a
  tour day MUST show no Rynke amount.
- **FR-005**: Tour days MUST count for the "Tour de Paris" badge category and,
  like every team event, for Team events attended, Big training and Attendances
  (feature 013 FR-010, FR-011), also after the qualification deadline; not for
  Technique training. Tour days MUST still earn no Rynke (FR-002).

**Tour de Paris team badges (amends feature 013)**

- **FR-010**: The team badges MUST get a category "Tour de Paris", a ladder with
  one badge per tour day, unit days (feature 013 FR-012). The tour always has 8
  days, so the ladder MUST be 1 to 8. Until Phase 2, the badges MUST carry
  stand-in names ("Tour-Tag 1" to "Tour-Tag 8"), stand-in descriptions ("Tag 1 der
  Tour de Paris – der Ort folgt") and the team placeholder picture, and MUST be
  fully usable that way: earned, shown, told and stuck into the album.
- **FR-011**: The badge for tour day n MUST be earned when at least one listed
  rider has an attendance at n tour days of the season, and MUST be dated with the
  date of the earliest tour day by which some rider reached n.
- **FR-012**: Tour badges MUST behave as every team badge (feature 013 FR-014,
  FR-030 to FR-032): shown under "Team-Abzeichen" only, worked out from listed
  riders only, never credited to or naming a rider, taken back silently when the
  attendances no longer reach them, and each new one told once to each listed
  rider.
- **FR-013**: The category MUST show its progress as the days still to go towards
  the next day badge ("Noch 3 Tage"), counted from the rider with the most tour
  days, without saying who that is.
- **FR-014**: Phase 2 MUST be able to replace the names, descriptions and pictures
  without changing how tour badges are earned or what was already earned. Names
  stay German only (feature 013). Nothing in Phase 1 or Phase 3 MUST wait for
  Phase 2.

**The season album: making it**

- **FR-020**: The maintainer MUST be able to make the season albums with one
  command on their own computer. Making albums MUST NOT be possible from the app,
  by anyone. Phase 1 MUST NOT upload albums anywhere (Phase 3 adds the upload).
- **FR-021**: The command MUST read the production data read-only: it MUST NOT
  change any stored data, MUST NOT call Strava, and MUST NOT notify anyone.
- **FR-022**: The command MUST write one album per listed rider at the time it
  runs, and none for anyone else.
- **FR-023**: The command MUST take a cut-off date, by default today in Berlin;
  every figure, badge and event in the albums MUST be as of that date.
- **FR-024**: The command MUST stop with an explanation when the configured season
  start lies after the cut-off date's season (the season start already moved).
- **FR-025**: The albums MUST be written to one folder inside the repository that
  git ignores, so they can never be committed (constitution Principle I); a check
  MUST keep that folder ignored.
- **FR-026**: The same data and cut-off date MUST give the same albums: where and
  how much each sticker is rotated and moved MUST follow from the rider and the
  badge, not chance.
- **FR-027**: Making the albums for 60 riders MUST take less than 15 minutes on an
  ordinary laptop.

**The season album: what it shows**

- **FR-030**: Each album MUST be an A4 portrait PDF for printing: the summary
  first, then the sticker pages, nothing cut off at the page edge. It MUST meet
  the print requirements FR-050 to FR-057.
- **FR-031**: The first page MUST show the album's title (stand-in "Rynke-Album"),
  the season, the rider's first name, their Training and Team Rynke at the cut-off
  date with the coin (feature 012), and the number of team events they attended.
- **FR-032**: The second page MUST sum up the team's season: the team's Training
  Rynke together (feature 016), and every team event of the season by date with
  kind, name and number of attending riders, the rider's own attendances ticked,
  tour days included. A list too long for the page MUST go on to the next page.
- **FR-033**: The summary MUST show only the rider's own figures and figures of the
  whole team; it MUST NOT name another rider or show another rider's figures
  (feature 004 FR-020).
- **FR-034**: When it runs, the command MUST say how many albums it wrote and
  where, and that the folder must be deleted once the albums are handed out; if
  the folder already holds albums, it MUST say how old they are.
- **FR-035**: The sticker pages MUST hold exactly one slot per badge of the
  catalog, personal categories first, then team categories, each category under
  its name, in the order of the app's gallery (feature 013 FR-002).
- **FR-036**: An earned badge MUST be stuck onto its slot's printed placeholder
  as a sticker: its picture as in the app (feature 013) on a white die-cut edge
  with a subtle gloss highlight and no glitter, turned by up to ±7° and moved off the
  placeholder's centre, mostly by up to 1.5 mm, in about one slot in three by
  2 to 4 mm so the placeholder's edge shows on one side; with a realistic shadow
  (a tight dark contact shadow and a soft ambient one) and in about one slot in
  four a slightly lifted edge (a longer shadow on that side and a light catch on
  its rim). Placement and shine MUST follow from the rider and the badge
  (FR-026). All of it MUST stay vector (FR-054). Its name and the date earned
  MUST be printed below. A sticker MUST NOT overlap another slot or leave the
  page.
- **FR-037**: Every slot MUST have its placeholder printed on the page: a light
  disc with the dashed edge of the app's gallery outline for a badge still to
  earn (feature 013 FR-002), the size of a sticker. A badge not earned MUST leave
  it empty, so it never looks like an earned badge that still has the placeholder
  picture (such as the stand-in tour badges), with the badge's name below; a surprise badge not earned MUST show "?" instead of its name and MUST
  NOT reveal its rule (feature 013 FR-015b).
- **FR-038**: Each group MUST say how many of its badges are stuck in, out of how
  many ("42 von 87").
- **FR-039**: Text in the album MUST come from the i18n catalogs in the rider's app
  language (feature 001 FR-028), addressing the rider as "du"; badge names stay
  German (feature 013).

**The season album: print-ready (2026-10-20)**

The album must go to a printer as it is: a home printer or a print shop, printed
double-sided, possibly bound or stapled.

- **FR-050**: Every page MUST be exactly A4 portrait, 210 × 297 mm (within
  0.5 mm), with the content at its final size, so it prints at 100 % ("actual
  size") without scaling.
- **FR-051**: Every page MUST keep a safe area: at least 12 mm from the top,
  bottom and outer edge, and a binding gutter of at least 20 mm on the inner edge.
  The margins MUST be mirrored for double-sided printing: odd pages (right-hand)
  have the gutter on the left, even pages on the right. No text, slot, sticker or
  shadow MUST reach outside the safe area.
- **FR-052**: Nothing MUST be printed to the page edge (no bleed): the paper stays
  white outside the safe area, so home printers, which cannot print to the edge,
  and print shops both print the album without trimming.
- **FR-053**: The album MUST have an even number of pages, ending with a blank
  page if needed, so double-sided printing keeps pages in pairs; a sticker
  category MUST NOT be split from its heading by a page break.
- **FR-054**: Everything that is a vector in the app MUST stay a vector in the
  PDF: text as real text, the badge pictures, the placeholders, the coin and the
  album's own decorations. Effects that a PDF can only hold as an image (such as
  blur filters on a picture) MUST NOT be used; shadows are drawn as vector shapes.
- **FR-055**: Text MUST use one font kept in the repository with a licence that
  allows embedding, embedded in the PDF, so the album looks the same on every
  computer and printer. Text MUST be at least 7 pt, lines at least 0.25 pt.
- **FR-056**: A picture that only exists as an image MUST be at least 300 dpi at
  its printed size.
- **FR-057**: The command MUST check every album it writes against FR-050,
  FR-053, FR-054 and FR-056 (page size, even page count, no image below 300 dpi,
  no image where a vector was expected) and MUST report any album that fails.

**Privacy**

- **FR-040**: The privacy page (feature 017) MUST say that at the end of the
  season the maintainer makes each rider's album from the stored data on their own
  computer, hands it only to that rider, and deletes it afterwards.
- **FR-041**: The album MUST hold no data the app does not already show to that
  rider; it needs no new consent version (constitution Principle I).

### Key Entities

- **Team Event**: as in feature 003, with the new kind "Tour de Paris".
- **Tour badge**: a team badge of the category "Tour de Paris", one per tour day
  (always 8), with stand-in name, description and picture until Phase 2.
- **Season album**: one rider's PDF for one season and cut-off date. Not stored by
  the app; exists only in the maintainer's ignored folder until deleted.
- **Slot**: one place per catalog badge on the sticker pages; empty (placeholder)
  or with the badge stuck in.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: Every tour day with a recorded attendance shows its team badge in the
  app the same day.
- **SC-002**: The maintainer makes all albums of a 60-rider season in one run of
  under 15 minutes, without touching the production data.
- **SC-003**: Every figure on an album's summary pages matches the app at the
  cut-off date (synthetic tests).
- **SC-004**: No album contains another rider's name or figures, and no album file
  ever reaches git.
- **SC-005**: A typical album (half the badges earned) is at most 15 MB, so it can
  be sent as an email attachment or chat file (measured in
  [research.md](research.md) R1: about 6 MB with today's pictures, all vector).
- **SC-006**: Riders who get their album say it looks like a sticker album, not a
  report (owner's judgement).

## Assumptions

- Features 003, 004, 013, 014, 016, 017 and 019 are in place.
- The tour lies after the qualification deadline and before 31 August; a new
  season starts on 1 September (feature 013).
- The maintainer has the access to the production data they already use for
  manual steps (CLAUDE.md), and a copy of the repository on their computer.
- Handing out the albums (email, chat, in person) happens outside the app and is
  not part of this spec; each rider gets only their own album.
- The tour always has 8 days (owner, 2026-10-20).
- The album's look follows the Claude Design mock-up linked above; its figures
  are a synthetic sample season.
- The PDF is in RGB colour, as browsers write it; print shops convert it to CMYK
  themselves. A PDF/X or CMYK file for offset printing is out of scope.
- History across seasons stays out of scope (feature 003): the album is the only
  record of a past season the rider keeps.
