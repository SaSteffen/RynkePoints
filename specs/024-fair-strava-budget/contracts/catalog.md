# Contract: Catalog Keys

| Key | de | en |
|---|---|---|
| `fetching.events` | `{done} von {total} Aktivitäten geholt` | `{done} of {total} activities fetched` |
| `fetching.import` | `Bisher {n} Aktivitäten geholt, weitere folgen` | `{n} activities fetched so far, more to come` |

Numbers are formatted with the catalog's locale (`formatNumber`, no decimals).
