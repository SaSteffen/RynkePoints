Peloton breakaway trial

Who rides in the breakaway, and how the road is scaled. Everything recomputes in the page; nothing here touches the app. All data sets are synthetic.

Chosen: “Gap to the bunch” (g = 0.4). The cycling definition: the breakaway is whoever has opened a gap in front of the group the median rider rides in. A lone rider far ahead stays a solo leader in front of a chase group instead of setting the scale. The breakaway is always smaller than half the team, a tight pack gets none, and the gap follows the team's median, so early and late in the season behave alike. The bunch spreads from its last rider to its front rider.
