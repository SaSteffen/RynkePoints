Ten team coins, every draft

For each team: the front, every draft of the back, and the back in colour where it's been coloured. "Your pick" marks the draft chosen for each team. Click a coin to see it large; "Gemini" shows the drawing before cleanup and lettering. Open this page from a checkout of the repo: it shows the files beside it, so after pnpm team-coins a reload shows the new state.

The model

Hamburg

Every coin keeps this frame: the double ring, Rynke on the front, and on the back the climb with four riders, the Eiffel Tower and the three icons. The drafts copy Hamburg's layout too: one big local object tying the scene together, everything standing on one band of ground. The coloured backs follow Hamburg's coloured back: gold ring, black band with gold letters, flat enamel colours.
